# 🛺 Mamar Rasta (মামার রাস্তা ৩ডি) — Free Bangladeshi 3D Driving Game

**Play Live Now:** 👉 **https://khadizafb120-sketch.github.io/mamar-rasta/**

**Mamar Rasta (মামার রাস্তা ৩ডি)** is a free browser-based 3D Dhaka, Bangladesh street driving, comedy & rickshaw modding game built by **Sabbir Codex & Majid (Sabbir Codex Development Team)**.

### ✨ Highlights
- **Old Dhaka (পুরান ঢাকা), Riverfront & Cable-Stay Bridge** with waving Bangladesh flags, Metro Rail, street **Michil (মিছিল)** & **U-Turn (ইউ-টার্ন)** drift zones
- **9 Playable 3D Vehicles:** Deshi Tesla Auto-Rickshaw, Super CNG Turbo, Bangladeshi Tractor Power, Dhaka Street Bike, Kawasaki Ninja H2R, Dhaka Leguna, Tesla Cybertruck, Lamborghini Aventador SVJ & Future Flying Car 2099
- **Rickshaw & Vehicle Modding Studio:** Custom body paint, swappable rear panels (Old Dhaka Rickshaw Art, SABBIR CODEX photo panel, BD Flag, Cyber Neon), canopies, neon underglow & roof mods
- **5 Game Modes:** Classic Old Dhaka, Old Dhaka Night, Monsoon Rain (3D rain), Free Roam, Hardcore (2× Cash)
- **5-Tier Graphics Presets:** Low, Medium, Max, Ultra & Ultra Max (2K Shadows)
- **Live Dhaka GPS Mini-Map** + **34 Viral Bangla Meme Clips** + **Satirical "দৈনিক যানজট" Newspaper Game Over Report**
